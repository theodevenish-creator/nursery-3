Our nursery

What we do, and what we don’t.

Two hours outdoors, every day

In the walled garden or on the Bourne fields, in all weather. We keep spare waterproofs and wellies in every size.

One key person, start to finish

Your child keeps the same key person from their settling-in days until they leave for school. You get their name before you accept a place.

Cooked here, every lunchtime

A three-week menu prepared on site by our cook, Marina. Allergies and diets are catered for, not worked around.

And three things we don’t do

No screens. No worksheets. No formal lessons before five. Children learn to read here when they are ready, and not a term sooner.

Rooms

Three rooms, three to five years apart.

  • 3 months – 2 years

    The Nest

    Twelve babies, four staff, one sleep room kept dark and quiet. Your child’s day comes home written down, not summarised.

  • 2 – 3 years

    The Orchard

    Sixteen places. Free-flow between the room and the garden all morning, so a child who wants to be outside can be.

  • 3 – 5 years

    The Long Room

    Thirty-four places, and the year before school. We hand over to Farnham primaries in person each July.

A day here

Predictable, on purpose.

Small children settle faster when the shape of the day never moves. Ours hasn’t changed since 1994.

  1. 7.30Doors open. Breakfast until nine.
  2. 9.15Outside: garden, fields or the walk to the Bourne.
  3. 11.45Lunch, cooked here, eaten together at the table.
  4. 12.30Sleep, or quiet time with a book.
  5. 16.00Stories at the window, then tea.
  6. 18.00Doors close.

Fees

Published, and inclusive.

One rate. Nappies, wipes, suncream, all meals and every trip are in it. We do not charge a registration fee.
